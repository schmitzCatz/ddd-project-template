= Domain Handbook
:description: A guided domain-driven design handbook for the project.

== Purpose

This handbook describes the business domain, its language, capabilities, boundaries, models, rules and decisions. It complements the arc42 architecture documentation without duplicating technical design.

== How to use this template

. Start with the domain vision and ubiquitous language.
. Explore business capabilities and subdomains before defining bounded contexts.
. Model bounded contexts, their relationships, and domain objects iteratively.
. Record domain events, business processes, rules and integration semantics.
. Capture unresolved questions and revisit the model as understanding evolves.

TIP: Each chapter contains guidance in a collapsible *Template guidance* section. Replace placeholder content with project-specific knowledge; remove guidance blocks before publication if desired.

== Modeling principles

* Business capabilities describe *what* the business can do.
* Subdomains describe areas of the *problem space*.
* Bounded contexts define boundaries of a consistent *solution model*.
* Aggregates protect business invariants, not arbitrary object graphs.
* Architecture implementation choices belong primarily in arc42.
