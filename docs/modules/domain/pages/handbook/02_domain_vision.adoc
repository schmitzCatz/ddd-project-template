== Purpose

Explain the business problem, desired outcomes and boundaries of the domain.

[%collapsible]
.Template guidance
====
*Guiding questions*

* What problem are we solving?
* Who benefits and how?
* What is explicitly outside the domain?
* What outcomes indicate success?

*Expected artifacts*

* Business context
* Domain mission statement
* Goals and measurable outcomes
* Stakeholders and actors
* Scope and exclusions
* Assumptions

*Modeling guidelines*

State the mission in business language. Avoid implementation decisions. Distinguish measurable outcomes from proposed features.
